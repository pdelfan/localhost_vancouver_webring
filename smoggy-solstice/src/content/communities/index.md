title: Communities

- [DWebYVR](https://dwebyvr.org/): main website for DWebYVR. Public MediaWiki install.
  - [DWebYVR Blog](https://writing.dwebyvr.org/): blog and email newsletter, Ghost-powered. Several subsites
  - [LOCALHOST Vancouver](https://writing.dwebyvr.org/localhost/): week-long set of local events alongside IETF120
  - [DWebYVR Calendar](https://lu.ma/dwebyvr): Luma-powered calendar for events
- [Luma Vancouver Calendar](https://lu.ma/vancouver): aggregate calendar of events in Vancouver
- [Z-Space](https://z-space.ca/): a shared studio space at 505 Hamilton managed by the Z-Space Arts and Technology Society
- [Vancouver AI Community](https://lu.ma/future-proof?tag=ai%20meetup) Luma calendar
- [van-techies](https://lu.ma/van-techies): A social group of tech folks in Vancouver. Come for the vibes, stay for the nerd snipes.
